By Rizwan Ahmad

The typical process when you put your home on the market for sale is to prep and make repairs to attract buyers, expending a great deal of time, money, and energy before you ever list. In addition, sellers face the inconvenience of showings, paying high real estate commissions, professional fees, and closing costs. 

There’s another option for sellers who prefer to save themselves from the headaches and uncertainties of a traditional listing yet work with a professional to handle the paperwork and keep more cash at closing. So read on as we explore how to sell your Milwaukee house without spending any money.

Additionally, you’ll need to keep your house pristine during the listing period so buyers can show up anytime for an impromptu walk-through. No Repairs

If you’re afraid to find out what a professional inspector may reveal about the condition of your home, fear not; a direct sale will allow you to sell your Milwaukee house without spending any money on costly repairs. Disclosure laws make you responsible for any issues with the property, even if discovered after the sale in some cases. There’s no way to guarantee your house will sell, whether listed as an FSBO or with an agent. If you’re considering selling as an FSBO to save the commissions for yourself, you should be aware statistics show your chances of selling faster and at market value are higher with an agent.
