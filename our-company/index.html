About Kustom Property Solutions

Kustom Property Solutions is a professional, full service real estate solutions firm that buys and sells properties throughout the greater Milwaukee, WI area. We provide win-win solutions to help homeowners get out of their sticky situations… like Foreclosure, owning a burdensome property, probate, or anything else. At Kustom Property Solutions, we focus on providing you with a solution to your situation so you can continue to do the things you love. Founded by Rizwan Ahmad, Kustom Property Solutions is excited to be part of the area’s renaissance and we aspire to continue contributing to the economic rejuvenation of Milwaukee and its surrounding communities.

Rizwan (Riz) is a second generation real estate investor, who at the young age of 21 took on the challenge of home building and successfully built his first house from the ground up. Since that time he has managed multiple new construction and full rehab projects over the last 17 years.

For years, Riz and Wajiha dreamed of creating their own real estate business, but didn’t know how it would work. They just knew they had to start somewhere; so after running a multimillion dollar Power Equipment Dealership for more than 16 years they just jumped right in, and decided to make their dream a reality.

Riz has been a part of the local community in New Berlin for more that 30 years. He has been very active in the community and has taken a keen interest in the betterment and training of youth. Riz served as the National Vice President of AMYA an international youth organization.

Since its inception, Kustom Property Solutions has passionately pursued the goal to help hundreds of people in our community find an answer to their real estate needs. Through the years, they’ve developed a solid foundation of real estate knowledge, with the integrity to follow up on promises and make successful deals happen. Our organization and founders has been an upstanding member of the greater Milwaukee community for years
